Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Kitchen Table Rummy is a card game for playing Rummy 500 with family and friends. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short answer: we don't collect any.

The short version

  • We don't collect, store or sell any personal data. We run no servers.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • Online games run through Apple's Game Center.
  • Your stats and settings stay on your device.

Online games (Game Center)

To play on separate devices, the app uses Apple's Game Center. When you start or join an online game, Game Center shares your Game Center nickname with the other players in that game, and stores the game itself (the cards, moves and scores) with Apple so everyone's device stays in sync. Game Center also sends the "your turn" notifications.

That data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't operate a server and can't see your games, your nickname or your Apple Account.

If you choose "Find random players", Game Center matches you with other players. There is no chat. Other players only see your Game Center nickname and your moves.

Stored on your device

Your wins, losses, game history, head-to-head records, table color, rule preferences and any pass-and-play game in progress are saved in the app's storage on your device. They're never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app is made for families and is fine for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. Parents can manage Game Center, including multiplayer with strangers, in Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → Game Center.

Changes to this policy

If this changes (for example, if a future version adds optional ads), we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version is released.
