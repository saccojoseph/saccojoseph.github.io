Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Kitchen Table Setback is a card game for playing Setback (also called Pitch) with family and friends. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short answer: we don't collect any ourselves. The free version shows ads provided by Google.

The short version

  • We don't collect, store or sell any personal data. We run no servers.
  • No accounts and no analytics. We never track you across other apps or websites.
  • The free version shows a non-personalized banner on the home screen and a short ad after each finished game, served by Google AdMob. A one-time purchase removes all ads.
  • Computer players run entirely on your device.
  • Online games run through Apple's Game Center.
  • Your stats and settings stay on your device.

Online games (Game Center)

To play on separate devices, the app uses Apple's Game Center. When you start or join an online game, Game Center shares your Game Center nickname with the other players in that game, and stores the game itself (the cards, moves and scores) with Apple so everyone's device stays in sync. Game Center also sends the "your turn" notifications.

That data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't operate a server and can't see your games, your nickname or your Apple Account.

If you choose "Find random players", Game Center matches you with other players. There is no chat. Other players only see your Game Center nickname and your moves.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a small banner on the home screen and a short full-screen ad after a game is finished. There are never ads while you're playing a hand. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, are set to the general-audience (G) content rating, and are non-personalized: the app never asks to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier isn't available to Google. To show and measure ads, Google's software may receive information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device and app details, and whether you saw or tapped an ad. Google handles that data under Google's Privacy Policy and explains it at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the United Kingdom), the app asks for your ad consent the first time you open it. You can change that choice at any time in the app's Settings under Ads.

The Remove ads purchase turns off all ads for good. Purchases are handled by Apple, and we never see your payment details.

Stored on your device

Your wins, losses, bids, game history, records with the people you play, your name, table color, rule preferences and any game in progress on this device are saved in the app's storage on your device. They're never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app is made for families and is fine for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. Ads are limited to the general-audience (G) rating and are never personalized. Parents can manage Game Center, including multiplayer with strangers, in Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → Game Center.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version is released.
